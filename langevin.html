Langevin Monte-Carlo

Research software from my doctoral work on the zero-temperature phases of an XXZ spin chain coupled to independent local dissipative baths.

Dissipation, ordering and transport.

Bosonization maps the quantum spin chain onto a classical field theory in space and imaginary time, with bath-induced interactions that are long-ranged along imaginary time. Langevin sampling helps explore the transition from a Luttinger liquid to a gapless spin density wave at incommensurate magnetization, or to a gapped antiferromagnet at zero magnetization.

The package provides C++17 header libraries and a native Python interface for both models, including initial simulations, checkpoint continuation and measurements of field roughness and ordering.
